# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (v2.1).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity, experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others’ private information (including secrets and credentials) without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the maintainer listed in `pyproject.toml`. Maintainers will review and take action they consider appropriate, including warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban.

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces and when an individual is representing the project.
